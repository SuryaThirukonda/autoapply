The form runs inside a cross-origin iframe: the outline may show nothing. Use the `computer` tool (screenshot,
click, type) and `tabs`. Steps: resume upload, profile, questions, EEO, review. iCIMS shows an hCaptcha before
the final submit on most tenants: fill everything, then park `ready_for_you`. Never type into a field addressed
to robots (a honeypot).
